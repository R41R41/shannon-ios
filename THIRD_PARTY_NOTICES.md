## Hermes Agent

Shannon's skill sleep (Syzygy ADR-0008 stage 2) adapts review rules and skill-provenance ideas from
[Hermes Agent](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent) by Nous Research: what never to capture
and the lesson layer of `agent/background_review.py` (`_DO_NOT_CAPTURE_BLOCK`, `_LESSON_LAYER_BLOCK`),

scan of written text (`services/api/src/mind/memory/skillDocument.ts`, `skillMemory.ts`); and, in tree recall,
