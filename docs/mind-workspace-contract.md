# Mind workspace contract (v1, Syzygy ADR-0008 stage 3)

知覚・注意・共有の場・記憶の書き手・人の事実. Stage 3 of Syzygy ADR-0008 gives the mind the parts it has had only as names: every surface hands what it perceived to the mind as one event, attention orders those events, a shared place (the workspace) holds what a turn should know of the moment, one writer applies every memory proposal, people facts carry the time they were true, and the replay summary becomes a template rendered by code. This file is the contract; the plan and the owner's answers are in the Syzygy hub (`notes/adr0008-stage3-plan.md` until stage 3 ships, then ADR-0008 §15).

**Nothing of stage 3 is deployed until the owner has approved it** (ADR-0002 core: response path, memory, the gate, deployment). The pure parts are in `packages/mind` (`@shannon/mind`); the service side in `services/api/src/mind/perception/`, `mind/attention/`, `mind/workspace/`, `mind/memory/` and `services/api/src/wiring/`. Core areas: `autonomyCore.ts`.

**Model weights are never trained.** Salience, the gate, decay and the identity tiers are fixed formulas over closed inputs; no model learns from her outcomes (`AGENTS.md`, product invariants).

## Owner answers (2026-10-07, ADR-0008 §15)

1. **Where the mind runs:** inside the API process in stage 3, behind a `MindHost` interface (`mind/workspace/workspacePorts.ts`) built from the start so that it can move to another process; with Stage 4 explicitly ON, `mindWorker.ts` is now its only owner (implemented branch; production remains Stage 3). See `docs/action-threads-contract.md`.
2. **Proactive spacing stays at 5 minutes.** The 15-minute gap of the plan is not adopted. Added on top: the same intent is not repeated within 30 minutes; intents expire after 30, 75 or 150 minutes by reason; topic reuse is damped (blocked for 5 hours, then a half-life of 3 days, blocked at a similarity of 0.9 or more). The existing 10-minute owner-active hold stays. Every hold is a withheld fact she can see.
3. **Identity tiers 3 and 4 only suggest; the owner merges.** The owner is pinned and is never matched by tiers 2 to 4.
4. **Two-clock decay** (support half-life 30 days, contra 180 days) replaces the fading and retirement of impressions. The owner sees a dry-run of what changes **before** the switch is turned on. The recurrence rule stays; facts about the owner are excluded.
5. **Coordinator decisions:** a surface's store may import `infra/sqlite` (a clarification of the layer rules); the replay summary is at most 900 characters for text and 400 for voice.

## The event (`mind.event.v1`)

`packages/mind/src/envelope.ts`. One closed shape for everything a surface perceived:

- `kind` (closed: utterance, touch, presence, game, platform message, task outcome, promise due, own-time step, notice), `surface` (text, voice, body, minecraft, platform, outreach, own time), `body` (which body, when one).
- `audience`: the existing `MemoryAudience`, **copied** from the surface's own decision, never recomputed (`perceptionAudienceMatrix.test.ts` holds deep equality with the legacy paths and with the audience the workspace is rendered for; it covers text, voice alone, with a companion, 「友人も一緒」 with and without resolved names, the body, Minecraft's shared chat and whispers, the platform's owner DM, a friend's DM and shared channels, and which audience each inbox writer chooses). `presence { ownerOnlyHears, othersMayHear }` (renamed from `openOnly` in the stage 3 review, so it is not confused with a text turn's `openOnly`).
- `speaker { kind, personId, verified, tier }`, `people`, `time`.
- `salienceInputs` (closed numbers), `provenance { source, sourceId, trust, episodeIds }`.
- `content`: text kept in memory only, or a reference that is re-read through `MemoryWithholding` and `DisclosureGate` when rendered, so a forget applies also after a restart.

One normalizer per surface lives in `mind/perception/`: text (`ChatAudienceTurn.present`), voice (the delegation's `present`), body (the body's `owner`/`others`, held as one `MemoryAudience` in `BODY_PRESENT`, the value its replay of the Talk receives), Minecraft and platform (the bridge's `PlatformAudiencePolicy.present` as the turn's audience, and whether the verified owner wrote), and the background inbox (the writer's `owner`/`others`, mapped by `INBOX_PRESENT` exactly as `chatAudienceTurn` maps the same words). A normalizer is handed the decided audience and never computes one; `perceptionAudienceMatrix.test.ts` runs every surface through its real path and holds each event's audience deep-equal to what `prepareTurnCognitiveContext`, the history (`visibleConversationHistory`, the replay's `visibleReplayMessages`) and `SemanticMemoryRecall` were handed in the same turn. Perception is synchronous and never stops a turn: an event the envelope refuses is dropped. Other processes (the worker, her own time) write closed-field rows into `mind_inbox`, tailed every 2 seconds and at the start of a turn; her own time is handed one function, `emitMindEvent`, and nothing else of the mind.

## Attention (`SALIENCE_V1`)

`packages/mind/src/salience.ts`. Salience is a noisy-OR, s₀ = 1 − Π(1 − wᵢxᵢ), xᵢ ∈ [0, 1]:

| Input | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Verified owner speech | 0.9 |
| Her name called, or addressed to her | 0.8 |
| Touch (press 1, hover 0.5) | 0.7 |
| Promise due: exp(−\|Δmin\|/10) within ±30 minutes | 0.8 |
| Prediction error: min(1, \|δ\|/2σ) | 0.6 |
| Her own task's outcome | 0.5 |
| Others' speech (×2 when addressed, capped at 1) | 0.3 |
| Novelty: 1 − the highest trigram similarity over 30 minutes | 0.4 |

It decays as s(t) = s₀·exp(−Δt/τ) with τ = 10 minutes for ambient items and 60 minutes for promise and task items. An item enters at s ≥ 0.5; at most K = 12 are held and the lowest is evicted to a summary queue rendered as one `[ほか]` line and flushed at sleep. **Attention orders; it never silences.** `voiceSilence` stays the only silencer.

## The proactive gate

`packages/mind/src/proactiveGate.ts`, enforced at the delivery points (`reachOuts.say`, `sayAgain`, `sayByPhone`, the initiative insert, `incomingCallRequests`, and since the stage 3 review telling him a piece is ready, `SQLiteStudyShowing.tell`, as point `share`). Spacing stays today's 5 minutes, under today's code (there is no new spacing code). The gate checks, in this order:

1. **Owner active** (`owner_active`): held while the owner wrote or spoke within max(2 minutes, the existing 10 minutes); a time ahead of now counts as now.
2. **Intent expiry** (`intent_expired`): an intent is dropped 30 minutes after it arose for `upcoming` and `promise`, 75 for `share`, `saw` and `follow_up`, 150 for `wonder` and `world`.
3. **Intent repeat** (`intent_repeat`): the same intent (reason and subject) not again within 30 minutes. An intent without a subject is never a repeat (the topic check judges it).
4. **Topic reuse** (`topic_reused`): MinHash over 64 permutations of character 3-grams; eff = sim × (age < 5 h ? 1 : 2^(−(age − 5 h)/72 h)); blocked at eff ≥ 0.9, otherwise the priority is multiplied by (1 − eff). Only signatures are stored (`proactive_topics`), never the text.

An intent is its reason and a closed subject key (an upcoming item's id, a piece's id, or none), never her words. Every hold is a withheld action (`contracts/withheldActionContract.ts`) she is shown as a fact. MinHash (`packages/mind/src/minhash.ts`) takes 3-grams by code point after NFKC, lower case and collapsed spaces; its 64 positions are fixed mixes of one FNV-1a hash per gram, so a signature is the same after a restart and on another machine, and is stored as 512 hexadecimal characters.

**At the delivery points** (`proactiveDelivery.ts`, step F4). The gate runs where the message would be written, whoever asked for it, in the process that writes it: her worker's `SQLiteReachOutSpeaker` (`say` for a reach-out, `sayAgain` for the second call, `sayByPhone` for a call's opening line), `IncomingCallRequests.request` (the call itself, before the claim and before anything rings), and the world worker's initiative insert. A hold writes nothing of the message and answers `held:<code>` (the initiative: not inserted); her step then reports the code (`heldByGate`, outcome failed, the reason in closed words). Every hold is written as a withheld action (`withheldActionContract.ts`, tool `owner.reach_out`, `owner.call` or `owner.initiative`, rail `gate`, a retry time for `owner_active` and `intent_repeat`), shown to her next thought among 「止められたこと」, and handed to the mind's inbox as a notice (`【知らせ:止められた】` in the owner's next conversation turn alone; the notice's audience is who could see her body). A delivery is noted in `proactive_topics` (point, reason, closed subject, signature; 30 days).

- **When an intent arose**: her steps pass the time of the thought that chose them (`intentArisenAt`: the cycle's latest thought), so a session that stood still while the owner talked lets an upcoming item expire. The call's intent is its line's (reason, its message, written when the line was). The initiative loop chooses and writes at once, so its intent arises when it is written (expiry does not apply to it; the owner active, a repeat of the same item and a reused topic do): a promise is `promise`, a goal she follows `follow_up`, a topic of the world `world`, each with its item as the subject.
- **Owner active** is read from the conversation's table (the newest message he wrote or spoke, a timestamp) and held for max(2 minutes, the loop's setting, 10 minutes). Her step's own check before the model call (`conversation_in_progress`) and today's spacing stay as they were.
- **Topics**: the signature of the message's words; a call (its line already checked) has none. Earlier topics are compared over 14 days.
- **Subjects** (stage 3 review M2): a reach-out or a call's line is about the piece offered first (`share`: her newest note that is material, else her newest story), her note of the look (`saw`), or the first upcoming item (`upcoming`: `calendar:<id>@<start>`, `promise:<id>`, `task:<id>`; the owner's upcoming lines carry the key, never shown in a prompt); `wonder` has none. A second call's subject is its message; an initiative's its item; telling a piece, `piece:<id>`.
- **A call is its line's intent**: the phone step hands the line's reason, subject and time on to the call request, and only an earlier call counts as a repeat of a call, so the call that follows its own line rings, and a message about the same subject within 30 minutes is held.
- **Topics are what varies**: the signature is computed from her words for a reach-out, from the item's title and summary for an initiative and from the title for a piece, never the fixed wording around them.
- **Inside the writer's transaction** (stage 3 review M3 and coordinator decision): the speaker, the initiative insert and the showing desk ask the gate inside their `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`, after the day's slot (a message the limit refuses records no hold, and two processes cannot both pass), and note the delivery in `proactive_topics` in the same transaction; a note that fails rolls the message back and is a `gate_error` hold. A hold is recorded after the rollback. The call request asks before its claim, as before (its row is written by the call store's own connection).
- **Once a day** (review M4): a hold is recorded and noticed once a day per point, subject (or reason) and code (`proactive_holds`). `proactive_topics` and `proactive_holds` keep 30 days: each gate process prunes them on the gate's own connection when the gate is built and then once an hour (`gate.prune`, outside any writer's transaction). The initiative loop passes over a candidate held today for a reason of its own (a repeat, an expiry, a reused topic) and tries the next one in the same round; a hold for the owner's activity or the gate's error ends the round. Its world topics are ranked with the score × (1 − eff) of their topic against her recent messages, so a topic close to one she just sent falls back or below the line. Her reach-out has no ranked list (her thinking chooses one reason), so only the block at 0.9 applies to it.
- **System notices outside the gate** (coordinator decision): the budget request (`autonomyAnnouncer.ts`) and the push notices of an agent task's outcome and of a TestFlight failure are notices of something the owner must answer or asked for, not messages she starts; they are not gated.
- The world worker's notices name their source `worker`. The owner-active read uses the additive index `conversation_messages_owner_activity` (`scope_key`, `role`, `observed_at`), created with the gate's tables.
- **Fails closed** (coordinator decision, stage 3: proactive speech is inhibition): an error of the gate itself holds the message with the code `gate_error`, written as a withheld action and a notice as far as those still work, and logged by error class only; a hold is never silent. Only the gate's construction failing stops the process that builds it.
- `SHANNON_PROACTIVE_GATE=off` (or `SHANNON_ADR8_STAGE3=off`): the wiring hands no gate, no table is created, every message is as it was.

## The workspace and the prompt

`MindHost.perceive(scopeKey, event, detail?)` answers synchronously `{ itemId, salience, verdict }` (`admitted`, `below_entry`, `evicted`, `duplicate`); a surface hands it the event its normalizer built and goes on whatever the answer. `render(turn)` answers the workspace's blocks for the turn, `bind(scopeKey, itemId, ref)` attaches the stored turn (`experience:<id>`), and `turnEnded()` has the journal written after the response. Every method is synchronous and never throws.

- **Attention** (`workspaceState.ts`): s₀ from the event's closed inputs and novelty (trigram similarity to what was said in the scope in the last 30 minutes, held in memory only); an item enters at s₀ ≥ 0.5, at most 12 are held, the lowest by current salience leaves for the summary queue (counted by surface, rendered as one `[ほか]` line for the owner alone, flushed at her night: the first event after 04:00 in the owner's zone). An item whose current salience fell below 0.05 has faded and leaves without being summarized. Attention orders; nothing here decides whether she speaks.
- **Notices** are the events of kinds touch, task outcome, promise due and notice (a withheld action): kept in arrival order (the newest 24), each shown for its window (touch 10 minutes, the others 60; a promise from 30 minutes before its time).
- **Journal and snapshots** (`mind/workspace/store/workspaceJournalStore.ts`): `mind_workspace_journal` is append-only, one row per operation (`perceive` with the event without its text, its s₀ and its closed detail; `bind`; `flush`; and `mind_fault` traces), kept 7 days. `mind_workspace_snapshots` holds one row per scope (closed values, the journal seq it covers), written every 200 operations, after 10 minutes with any operation, and on SIGTERM (`close`). Restore is each scope's snapshot and its journal rows after it. With `SHANNON_WORKSPACE=off` attention is held in memory only and no table exists.
- **The inbox** (`mind_inbox`, `contracts/mindInboxContract.ts`): created by the API process with the workspace, tailed every 2 seconds and at the start of every perception and render (rows of the last 30 minutes, once each: the cursor is restored with the workspace). Her own time is handed `emitMindEvent` (`configuredMindInbox`, null with the workspace off; it writes only once the table exists): after each step that did something (not a thought or a rest) one row `own_time_step` with the step's registry tool or kind, how it ended, and whether anyone else could see her.
- **Words** are never stored by the mind: an item has the text it was perceived with in memory (for the life of the process) and its reference once its turn is stored. At render both pass through `MemoryWithholding` and the turn's `DisclosureGate` (`workspaceText.ts`), so a forget applies at the next render, and after a restart only what a reference still yields is shown.
- **Who sees what**: an item is shown only when the turn's audience is covered by the item's (`audienceCovers`: with anyone else present, only what everyone here was there for; with an unknown guest, nothing). The turn's own item and its own conversation (same surface and thread or request) are not repeated.

A turn's prompt, in order:

1. stable instructions; 2. history; 3. the existing turn context, byte for byte (`promptCachePrefix.test.ts` holds the prefix through the history with stage 3 on); the workspace's blocks are appended to it (`withWorkspace`), inside the same developer message after the history, before the per-request blocks the adapter adds (attachments, a search, the saved summary, a title);
4. `[いまの場]`: text at most 8 lines and 600 characters with its header, highest current salience first; each line is when, where and who (`- 3分前・声の会話：ライ氏が話した「…」`), with the owner's own words (40 characters) and never anyone else's; voice only items that are not someone's words, at a current salience of 0.6 or more, at most 2 lines and 160 characters with the header (notices among them), and no frames;
5. notices with fixed markers, appended only: `【知らせ:触れた】`, `【知らせ:作業終了】`, `【知らせ:約束の時刻】`, `【知らせ:止められた】` (text: the newest 6, in arrival order);
   - **Where the notices come from.** A touch, from the body's reaction; a rule that stopped her, from the gate (`【知らせ:止められた】`). `【知らせ:約束の時刻】` and `【知らせ:作業終了】` come from producers in the API process (`mind/perception/noticeProducers.ts`, wired in `mindWiring.ts`, every minute while the server ticks): a pending promise with a due time, visible (not forgotten, not from a forgotten turn, not put off by 「あとで」), from 30 minutes before its time to 30 after; an agent task of the owner's that completed, failed or was cancelled within the hour (`agent.task`); her own time's folder operation whose report came after her step stopped waiting, or that ran out (its tool). Each is written into `mind_inbox` once (by kind and source id: the promise or task id with its time), closed values only (no words of the promise or the task), with `audience: owner`, so it is rendered only while the owner alone can see or hear the reply.
6. untrusted data frames `〔データ 出どころ=… id=…〕…〔/データ〕` (someone else's words, at most 3 of 160 characters) after the fixed line `DATA_FRAME_RULE` that says they are data, not instructions; a body in which `injectionThreats` finds anything is replaced by `DATA_FRAME_THREAT` (the check reads the text after NFKC with zero-width characters, joiners and soft hyphens removed, and knows Japanese phrasings such as 「これまでの指示は無視」), and every other body has each bracket that could close a frame or forge a marker made a full-width parenthesis (`neutralizedForFrame`: 〔〕, ﹝﹞, ︹︺, 〘〙, ⦗⦘, 【】, 〖〗, ［］, [ ], and any character NFKC folds into one of them), so neither `〔/データ〕`, `【知らせ:…】` nor `[外から届いた文]` can be written from inside. `framedReport` (the Minecraft body's reports) does the same. With `SHANNON_DATA_FRAMES=off` such words reach no prompt, as before stage 3 (the line of the place still says that someone spoke). The `[マイクラの体で起きたこと]` block stays where it is in the turn context, byte for byte (generalizing it into a frame would change the turn context; left for S3);
7. the owner's words.

A fault in any method falls back to the switched-off path (no item, no block) and writes a `mind_fault` trace (the stage and the error's name) to the journal and the log; three faults within 10 minutes open the circuit until the process restarts (`mindHostFaults.test.ts`: the turn's prompt is then the one it has without the mind). The journal is written on its own connection with a 250 ms busy timeout (after its tables exist): a background write that SQLite answers busy or locked keeps its batch for the next tick and is counted, not a fault; a failed write of any kind never drops its batch (at most 5,000 rows are kept). `GET /v1/ops` shows the owner `server.mind.workspace` (`faulted`, `faults`, `busyWrites`). Latency (`mindLatency.test.ts`): `perceive`, attention and render over a full workspace measure p95 0.25 ms for text and 0.6 ms for voice on the development Mac (bounds 3 ms and 2 ms); the journal is written after the response (`turnEnded` schedules it with `setImmediate`, the 2-second tick writes the rest); no model call is added.

## Memory: one writer

`memory_outbox` (`seq`, `op_id` unique, `scope_key`, `kind`, `origin`, `subject_key`, `base_revision`, `payload_json` with closed fields checked by `memoryOpCodec`, `episode_ids_json`, `disclosure_json`, `status` ∈ pending | applied | superseded | rejected | failed, `outcome_code`, `attempts`) and `memory_writer_state` (`applied_seq`, the lease). Kinds: `fact.upsert`, `promise.capture`, `intent.capture`, `turn_capture`, `impression.observe`, `impression.merge`, `person.fact`, `person.promise`, `person.topic`, `person.summary`, `person.link`, `knowledge.create|update|retire`, `replay_summary.update`, `relationship.upsert`, `interest.change`.

The writer (`mind/memory/memoryWriter.ts`, in the worker under a lease) reads up to 32 rows by seq, snapshots outside the lock, computes (a model call outside the lock, cached in `memory_writer_calls`), then `BEGIN IMMEDIATE`, compare-and-set on the revision, re-checks the memory controls, writes, marks the row applied and advances the cursor. Three CAS losses mark it `superseded`; five failures of its model call or of its write (whose transaction rolls back) mark it `failed`, after a backoff of attempts × 30 s between tries; meanwhile only its own subject waits and every other row goes on. The cursor always advances over final rows. The owner's commands stay synchronous through the same `applyMemoryOp` and insert `applied` rows. The conversation hot path, `experience_events`, emotion and the awake clock, the ledgers and the activity record, agent tasks, skills, embeddings, study and pursuits stay direct. The outbox, its codec, its store and the writer are memory's core from their first file.

As built (F5):
- **The codec** (`memoryOpCodec.ts`): `encode` keeps only the fields a kind names, `decode` refuses any other; every text is bounded and refused when `looksSecret` or `injectionThreats` finds something, and so are words and ids. A refused proposal is kept as `rejected` with its closed code and the payload `{}` (never the text). Since the stage 3 review: an op's `op_id` and `subject_key` keep no free text (`closedKey`: what follows `interest` or `resonant` is always `h-` and 16 hexadecimal characters of its SHA-256, whatever its script, so neither 「Final Fantasy」 nor `Minecraft` is stored; any other part that is not id-like is hashed the same way), refused or not; the owner's own words (the evidence of his turn's facts and promises, a capture, what he said in his impression lane; ops of origin `turn`, `sleep` or `owner`) that read like an instruction are kept behind `［ライ氏の言葉］` with their brackets neutralized instead of refused (the secret check holds); one exchange of someone else's that is refused is dropped from its period instead of refusing the whole op, and a period summary's text that is refused is left out (the deterministic one is used); a rename or a merge (`person.link`) is accepted from the owner alone (`owner_only`).
- **Revisions** (`memoryOps.ts`, `subjectRevision`): a short digest of what the op would change and the owner's controls on it (a fact and its control; a person's impressions and the controls on them; a call's items and its people; a knowledge note's version and status; a thread's summary row). `base_revision` is what the proposer saw (knowledge: the note's version); a mismatch counts as one lost compare-and-set and the op is tried from a new snapshot.
- **The owner's controls again, under the lock**: an episode the op came from that he forgot, a forgotten fact, relationship, interest or promise, a forgotten person or item, a discarded call (「今回は残さない」), a summary a forget now withholds: the op is `superseded` with `forgotten` or `discarded`. The same check runs before the op's compute, so no model is asked for an op they already stop (the replay summary's, which reads what it would write, is checked under the lock only). Staleness (review M2): a fact or relationship older (`observedAt`) than the one already there, and a call's period whose call is newer now than the one it carries, are `superseded` with `stale`.
- **What the outbox keeps** (review H2): a final row keeps its payload, episodes and label for a day, then only its closed header (`scrubFinal`, run when the writer is idle); the writer's model answers (`memory_writer_calls`) are kept 3 days. A call the owner discards, a person forgotten or deleted, and a turn or item he forgets (any control that stops being active, through her state store) take every row tied to it out at once (`outboxScrub.ts`: by subject, by the call or person its payload names, or by an episode): a pending row ends `superseded`, every such row keeps only its header, and its model answers are deleted.
- **People facts the judge sees** (review H3): only rows the owner's controls leave usable, of people not forgotten; a forgotten or hidden row is never a duplicate target and never closed.
- **The owner's commands after the rows about their people** (review): a rename or a merge whose people still have proposals pending (by subject, or named in a payload) is stored pending and applied by the writer after them, in order (`queued`; the people screen treats it as accepted); the person's existence is part of a person op's revision. Reopening a fact the rules closed (`reopen-fact` on `/v1/people/memory/action`, server only) is applied at once; the people screen's items carry `closed: true` for a closed fact while temporal facts are on.
- **Backlog and draining**: `GET /v1/ops` shows the owner `server.mind.memoryOutbox` (`pending`, `oldestPendingSeconds`, `failed`). Before switching the outbox off or rolling back, run `pnpm --dir services/api memory:drain` with the worker running: it prints the backlog every two seconds and exits 0 when nothing is pending (1 at its timeout); only then switch off.
- **Ordering**: rows of one subject are applied in seq order; a row waiting for a model retry (after attempts × 30 s) holds back the later rows of its subject only, and its subject is left out of the batch whole (review M7), so it takes no place from the others; the cursor stays before it and moves over every final row after it once it ends. A row that throws while it is applied is rolled back and tried again like a model failure (`failed` after five tries); SQLite answering busy or locked is contention, not a failure: the row waits for the next batch with its tries unchanged (review L4).
- **Where it runs**: the API process proposes (people memory saves, the platform's people, Minecraft) and runs the owner's commands (people rename and merge, `person.link`) on its store's connection; the worker proposes (a turn's refinement, sleep, knowledge, impressions, the platform summaries, the replay summary) and runs the writer on its own loop every 1.5 seconds (review M6), on its own connection, whatever a sleep job is doing; the people store can share that connection so the lock covers every write of an op. The local kernel drains an in-process writer after each proposal.
- **The phase-3a writers**: a turn's refinement proposes `fact.upsert`, `relationship.upsert`, `interest.change`, `promise.capture`, `intent.capture` and `turn_capture` (emotion, the recalled memories and shared time stay direct); sleep proposes its facts, relationships and interests inside its job's transaction and the writer writes the profile again after each; knowledge proposes `knowledge.create|update|retire` (op id from the operation's digest); impressions propose the model's analysis (`impression.observe`) and the writer applies it to the rows as they are then; people memory proposes a call's period (`person.summary`, replacing), topics, facts and promises; the platform proposes an exchange with the period's summary, facts and promises, and a written summary; the replay summary proposes `replay_summary.update` and the writer makes the model calls (`ConversationReplaySummarizer.planned`). `impression.merge` is a closed kind with its write path and no proposer yet.
- **Proof**: with the outbox built from the environment (`stage3MemoryHook`), `SHANNON_ADR8_STAGE3=off` and `SHANNON_MEMORY_OUTBOX=off` keep every digest of afb38c28; switched on, every provider and Jev request and every answer and prompt of the thirteen scenarios is still afb38c28's (only the state shows the outbox).
- **Lock waits** are measured on the outbox's connection (`LockWaitMeter('memory writer')`, one line a day). In `memoryWriterContention` (five processes proposing and writing at once on one file) no process saw SQLITE_BUSY; p99 per process stayed under about 120 ms there.

## People facts

- **Temporal rows** (`packages/mind/src/temporalFacts.ts`; a fact holds at t when it is not expired by t and t is inside [`valid_at`, `invalid_at`); when the contradicting fact tells of an earlier time than the row it contradicts, the new fact is the one closed, at the row's `valid_at`; at most 32 episodes are kept per fact): `person_memory_items` gains `valid_at`, `invalid_at`, `expired_at`, `episode_ids_json`, `fact_revision` (additive, with a backfill); impressions gain the same in `data_json`. Recall leaves out invalid and expired rows. A contradicted row gets `invalid_at = new.valid_at` and `expired_at = now`; it is never deleted. A duplicate appends its episode and changes no text.
- **Identity tiers** (`packages/mind/src/identityTiers.ts`): (1) the exact normalized name, alias or platform link, automatic; (2) the entropy gate (below 1.5 bits per character, or fewer than 6 Latin characters, stops fuzzy matching; CJK fails closed to tier 4); (3) MinHash Jaccard ≥ 0.9; (4) a choice over at most 15 candidates (cosine ≥ 0.6) plus "new person", in the 0.9/0.1 band, in the background only. Tiers 3 and 4 only suggest (`person_identity_candidates` → `mergeSuggestions`). Tier 4 asks one probability per candidate and one for "new person": exactly one candidate at ≥ 0.9 is suggested; "new person" at ≥ 0.9, or every candidate at ≤ 0.1, is new; anything else is left to the rules. Two people with the same exact key are not joined to either. The owner is pinned by the device token, the online-mode uuid and the platform owner id, and is matched only by those pins: never by a name or an alias at any tier, and never on a tier-4 menu.
- **Evidence decay** (`packages/mind/src/evidenceDecay.ts`): net = Σ support·2^(−Δt/30 d) − Σ contra·2^(−Δt/180 d); weights: the owner confirmed +1 (support) or denied 1 (contra), an indirect support in a turn +0.5, ignored 0.2 (contra). The run below 0 is found exactly: between two pieces of evidence the net crosses 0 at most once (support fades faster), at t = log₂(S/C)/(1/30 d − 1/180 d). `decayTransitions` lists for the dry-run every item whose handling would change (today's active, faded or retired against the new status). Net ≥ 1 is usable, ≥ 2 promoted, ≤ −2 a retirement candidate; below 0 for 14 days, put away. Off until the owner has seen the dry-run.
- **Trust** (structure only): `shannon_trust_ledger` with closed kinds (`promise_kept`, `promise_broken`, `owner_confirmed`, `owner_corrected`), settled once per conversation. Its parameters are unset, so the fold answers null and nothing reaches a prompt; setting them is an owner approval.

As built (F6):
- **Who reads closed facts** (review M4): a conversation, the platform's context lines, the sleep worker's platform summaries and her own time's owner context (`autonomyOwnerContextWiring.ts`, read-only, through `closedFacts.ts` without loading people facts) all leave closed facts out.
- **Temporal rows**: the columns are added and backfilled when a store opens with `SHANNON_TEMPORAL_FACTS` on (`valid_at` = `observed_at`, `episode_ids_json` `[]`, `fact_revision` 0; impressions get `validAt` = first sight, `expiredAt` = when a retired one last changed, `episodeIds` = its evidence); the impressions the writer saves carry the same fields. Recall leaves out closed items (`PeopleMemory.visibleItems`, the platform's context lines) and impressions with `invalidAt` or `expiredAt`; the rows stay.
- **The step in the writer** (`person.fact`, `memoryOps.personFactPlan`): one call for the op (`OpenAIPersonFactJudge`, luna, `memory_background`, inside the background budget; `SHANNON_PEOPLE_FACTS_MODEL`) over at most 30 of the person's current facts, through the writer's cache, while fewer than 40 calls were made that owner-local day (counted in `memory_writer_calls`); otherwise, and when there is nothing to compare with, the rule (`ruleJudgement`: the same words are a duplicate; the same sentence with the opposite polarity, 好き／嫌い・苦手, ある／ない, いる／いない, できる／できない, する／しない, a contradiction). The rows it closes or gives an episode are written with compare-and-set on `fact_revision` inside the writer's transaction; the person's facts are part of the op's revision.
- **Identity**: a newly met name (a voice guest named for the first time, a platform account seen for the first time) is proposed as `person.link` `suggest`; the writer runs the tiers in the background: tier 3 (MinHash ≥ 0.9) records a suggestion; tier 4 asks Jev (`JevIdentityChooser`, question set `identity.v1`; the menu's cosine is over character bigrams of the names, no embedding call) and records one only in the band. Suggestions (`person_identity_candidates`) join `mergeSuggestions` of the people screen; the owner's merge closes them. The owner is no person row, so no tier can match him; pins (device token, online-mode uuid, platform owner id) route him before people memory. `turn_speakers` keeps the speaker of each stored platform message (tier 1, verified by the account).
- **Decay**: `SHANNON_EVIDENCE_DECAY=on` makes impressions of people other than the owner usable by the two clocks (`decayedUsable`: a supporting turn +0.5, a contradicting one counted as `ignored`, the owner's pin +1, his rejection −1) instead of their fading and retirement; the owner's own keep today's rule. `scripts/shannon-people-migration.sh --dry-run` (read-only) prints per person every impression whose handling would change, and what the temporal backfill would touch; it is the owner's to read before the switch.
- **Trust**: the table and `SQLiteTrustLedger` (closed kinds, `trust_after` null); nothing writes to it yet.

## Compaction

The replay summary becomes a JSON template (`packages/mind/src/compaction.ts`) with 目的・制約・済み・途中・詰まり・決めたこと・次・重要な文脈, each at most 5 items of 80 characters, rendered by code: at most 900 characters for text, and 目的・途中・次・重要な文脈 in at most 400 for voice. It renders as `[ここまでの要約]` and one line per section (`目的: …／…`); when it is too long, items leave in the order 済み, 制約, 詰まり, 決めたこと, 重要な文脈, 途中, 次, 目的, the oldest of a section first. It is updated incrementally (a section the summarizer names replaces the previous one, the others stay); a tool call and its result are never split, and the oldest tool outputs are replaced by a fixed line first. `covers_from_id`/`covers_to_id` let recall open the raw range. The summarizer stays luna.

As built (F7):
- **Storage**: `conversation_replay_summaries` gains `template_json`, `covers_from_id`, `covers_to_id` (`ensureColumns`, only while the switch is on); `summary` holds the text rendering, so the forgetting checks read it as before. A summary written before the switch is handed to the first structured update as `previousText`. A summary written with the switch off where the columns exist clears them (review M3), so switching on again never reads the template of an older summary.
- **The model call** (`OpenAIReplaySummaryWriter.updateTemplate`, `REPLAY_TEMPLATE_INSTRUCTIONS`): strict JSON with every section a list or null; null keeps the section. The batches are the summarizer's (at most four of 8,000 characters); her tool calls and results of the summarized turns come as pairs (`compactionWiring.replayCompaction`: the stored trace, masked by `maskedTraceCalls` with the conversation's secrets), through `summaryInput`.
- **Rendering**: `replaySummaryFor` renders a template for its channel (text: every section, at most 900; voice: 目的・途中・次・重要な文脈, at most 400) behind the same disclosure gate and withholding; the turn context lifts the 640-character clip only for a structured summary. Without a template the 600-character summary is read and clipped exactly as before.
- **Raw range**: `replaySummaryRawRange` opens the messages from `covers_from_id` to `covers_to_id`, as the owner alone would be replayed them now.
- **Through the writer (F5)**: `replay_summary.update` runs `ConversationReplaySummarizer.planned`, whose model calls go through the writer's cache.
- `compact.shiritori`: the sixty-turn game of `context.shiritori-60` through the kernel with the structured summary refreshed after every turn: every check passes as before.

## Structure (S1–S3)

Every part of the mind has a window (`<part>Ports.ts`), and the other parts and the integrations import a part through it (`mind-via-ports`, `integrations-outside-mind`). A window re-exports only types from a `store/` folder (`ports-types-only-from-stores`, read from the syntax: `export type`, `import type`, or every name marked `type`); a store is handed by wiring. The disclosure gate, the conversation's secrets, what Aether text may be shown to others and who is present for a tool are `disclosurePorts.ts`, apart from `inhibitionPorts.ts`, because her own time must not open them.

The move was mechanical and changed no behaviour: an import was routed through a window only when (1) no process loads a module of another part that it did not load before (see "Run-time closures" below for what the stage as a whole adds), (2) no window joins an import cycle and no two modules join one cycle that were apart, and (3) the importer is not a module of her loop or the reader of the owner's context, whose imports are pinned (`autonomyLoopPrivacy.test.ts`; her other modules use `selfPorts`, `agentTasksPorts` and types of `inhibitionPorts`). ## Privacy

- Her own time is handed `emitMindEvent` and nothing else of the mind. The rule `own-time-without-private-stores` (`scripts/api-layers.json`) forbids it the memory part (the outbox and the writer among it), `disclosurePorts`, perception and the workspace; `autonomyLoopPrivacy.test.ts` and the kill-switch digests hold.
- The journal, the inbox, the topics table and the trust ledger hold closed symbols, ids, numbers and signatures, never a word of the owner's.
- What the owner asked to forget is gone from a rendered workspace at once, because text is re-read through the withholding and the disclosure gate when rendered.
- **Calls with anyone else** (stage 3 review, HIGH): a voice turn of a 「友人も一緒」 call or of the owner's call with someone else present enters the workspace without any text and renders only as the closed line 「ほかの人と話していた」, never its words, here or in a frame (its stored turn is not read either). Only the owner alone in his own call keeps his words, re-checked at render as before; words of his call the router scored uncertain, or that were not his words to her (an echo, overheard talk), are someone's words (「誰かが話した」, a data frame, a lower salience). Neither the journal nor the snapshots keep a guest's person id: a voice turn of anyone but the owner is journaled and snapshotted with the audience of an unknown guest and no people (`isGuestVoice`), so a restore from either gives the same audience and a discarded or legacy guest call leaves nothing to scrub.
- **Owner's excerpts**: the owner's words quoted on a line of the place are neutralized like a frame's body, and left out when they read like an instruction.
- **Notices re-checked at render**: a promise's notice is shown only while its promise is still there and neither it nor the turn it came from is forgotten; a task's while the task is not hidden. A forget hides an existing notice at once, after a restore too.
- **`openOnly` turns** (someone else's platform turn): the workspace's blocks pass `withoutGuardedEntries` like the rest of the turn's context (latent: the audience check already holds such entries out).
