# Shannon development instructions

This repository contains the current Shannon iOS app, its API, background workers, and the Mac Agent Host used for user-directed self-improvement.

## Instruction authority

For every task, use this order when sources disagree:

1. The user's current request.
2. This file.
3. `README.md` for the canonical-document index and product policy.
4. `docs/architecture.md` and `docs/DESIGN.md` for the current implemented system and UI contract.
5. `docs/self-improvement-runbook.md` and `docs/BRANCHING.md` for verification, deployment, and worktree lifecycle.

HTML files under `docs/archive/` are design history and operational evidence. They are useful context, but they are not current implementation authority unless the user explicitly names one.

## Product invariants

- Keep the internal product, target, and scheme names as `ShannonApp` / `ShannonDesk`; the user-facing app and character display name is `シャノン`.
- Ordinary chat must not wait for emotion updates, memory extraction, sleep consolidation, web exploration, push delivery, or self-improvement work.
- v1 uses a fixed LLM. Personality, emotion, interests, user knowledge, recalled memories, and current task state are prompt/shared-state inputs. No model-weight training (no per-user fine-tuning, no LoRA, no training of a model on her outcomes); value tables that change choices from outcomes are allowed (Syzygy ADR-0008), as are prediction errors that move her feeling (Syzygy ADR-0006 stage 2). The prediction ledger, the choice ledger, the value table and the decision traces are described in `docs/cognition-ledger-contract.md`; they hold closed symbols and numbers only, never a word of the owner's, and the loop is handed functions, never their stores. Stage 2 of ADR-0008 (Jev's tool menu, the folded read tools, difficulty tiers, the turn verifier, skills, tree recall) is described in `docs/skill-and-routing-contract.md`; `SHANNON_ADR8_STAGE2=off` restores ca7cf519 byte for byte. Stage 3 (perception, attention, the workspace, the memory outbox and its one writer, people facts with validity, identity tiers that only suggest, the proactive gate, structured compaction) is described in `docs/mind-workspace-contract.md`; `SHANNON_ADR8_STAGE3=off` restores afb38c28 byte for byte.
- Shannon's persona, the suite role dictionary, and the description of what she can and cannot do have one source: `packages/cognition/src/persona.ts` and `capabilities.ts`. Do not restate them in a path-specific prompt. A change that adds or removes an ability updates `capabilities.ts` and `services/api/tests/shannonCapabilityEvidence.test.ts` in the same change. Fixed sentences spoken as Shannon use that voice (`ボク`, `ライ氏`, です・ます); status and error text stays neutral.
- Aether data enters Shannon only through the bounded projection and, while `SHANNON_AETHER_API_URL` and `SHANNON_AETHER_SHANNON_TOKEN` are set, through her Aether tools (Syzygy ADR-0007 revised, contract `syzygy.aether-shannon-access.v1`, `docs/aether-access-contract.md`): the owner's documents are searched and read only while the owner alone can see or hear the reply; with anyone else present, and in her own time, only inside 「シャノン用フォルダ」 (enforced in `shannonToolGate.ts`). By the owner's decision of 2026-10-06 her own time may also read his documents with `ownerDocs.*`, for one of her active pursuits, only while `SHANNON_AUTONOMY_OWNER_DOCUMENTS=on`; what a session writes after reading his documents or a shared Notion page is marked (`ownerInformedMarks.ts`), is not read with anyone else present, and is not given to her own sessions that are not marked (option (a)); a marked session holds her messages while others are present and reaches nobody else, also with the switch off for a Notion page. She writes freely only inside that folder; the owner's documents change only through proposals in Aether's inbox (v2: new-note, append, update, delete). She never silently rewrites his Aether.
- Bounded autonomy (Syzygy ADR-0002). Shannon may propose a change from background activity, including a change to herself. Applying core or production changes needs the exact owner approval; permitted non-core changes in her isolated workspace follow the existing free-work gate and the Stage5 deterministic checks.
  - Core needs the owner's approval: purpose, base prompt and persona, emotion source, authentication, memory storage and forgetting, the approval and budget guard, the activity record, the response path, and deployment. `services/api/src/mind/inhibition/autonomyCore.ts` maps these areas to paths. The same applies to Aether and Voyager app code, the owner's documents and tasks, and anything that reaches a person other than the owner (sending, posting, publishing).
  - Free without approval, and nothing else: her own folder on the owner's Windows PC and Mac, her non-core code in the Azure VM workspace kept apart from the production service, her own study (with Aether configured her stories live in Aether's 「シャノンの書斎」 and her notes in 「シャノン用フォルダ」, and Shannon keeps their stages, versions and the owner's reactions, `studyAether.ts`; without it the study is Shannon's SQLite study, `studyStore.ts`; stories are never put in the PC or Mac folder), and her own task list in Voyager.
  - Never: spending money beyond the daily model/API budget, contacting or publishing to others without approval, careless external access, impersonation, writing what the owner asked to keep secret into a work or a note (by the owner's decision of 2026-10-02 her own stories and notes, which the owner reads, may be about him and the people he knows; showing a work to anyone else needs his approval, except reading her stories and notes, unshown drafts included, aloud in a conversation with others present, which Syzygy ADR-0007 revised allows by the owner's decision of 2026-10-06; the app's study screen still hides an unshown story), changing core without approval, and self-replication. By the owner's definition (Syzygy ADR-0002 addendum, 2026-10-07) self-replication is Shannon making a copy of this architecture's code as a whole in a place the owner cannot reach; copying her code, memory or persona to run on a machine, account or service the owner does not manage stays forbidden as before. Running actions in parallel inside the system the owner manages, under the same self, the same gate (`authorizeAutonomousAction` / `authorizeToolCall`), the same activity record and the same daily budget and stop switch (action threads), is not replication; Stage 4 action threads are implemented and enabled in the recorded production baseline `8200c128`; the existing action gate owns every lease and acknowledgement.

Never give it the conversation keys, a second runner, or a way to start processes. By the owner's decision of 2026-10-02 (Syzygy ADR-0003) she paces her own day: within the daily budget she runs in sessions at times of her choosing (`autonomyLoopPacing.ts`), each until she rests with `rest`, which says in how many minutes she comes back and why, in her own words, or until a bound stops it (what one session may spend, its wall clock, its steps, the sessions of a day, the shortest rest; `SHANNON_AUTONOMY_SESSION_*`), with the reason in her record either way. With Stage4 ON, the existing scheduler runs up to three distinct action threads, prioritizes respond admission and stops/reconciles work under its leases; with Stage4 OFF, a session stands still while the owner is talking and continues afterwards with the same plan. Every thinking step of a session ends with the facts of her budget as the server counted them. Work the owner calls her for (conversation, agent tasks, a look he asks for) is never charged to this budget. `SHANNON_AUTONOMY_PACING=cycles` restores the fixed cycles; together with `SHANNON_AUTONOMY_OWNER_CONTEXT=off` it restores the loop exactly as it was. Its prompts are built from her pursuits, her study, public reading, allowed Notion reading, and reader feedback, and, by the owner's decisions of 2026-10-02, from these things of the owner's: (1) her own note of what she saw through her body's camera (`study_pieces.origin = 'room_look'`), material for her thinking and her stories (while `SHANNON_AUTONOMY_OWNER_CONTEXT` is off, with the earlier rule line that it is never written into a story as seen); and (2) the owner's upcoming items, so that she can speak up about what is near: calendar events the apps share, promises and reminders she remembers that have a due time, and dated Voyager tasks when that integration is configured. ## Repository map

- `Shannon.xcworkspace`: sole interactive Xcode entry point for both Apple apps and their shared local package graph.
- `apps/ios`: Swift package containing Shannon's SwiftUI screens, models, local cache, and tests.
- `apps/ios-app`: signed iOS application shell and Xcode project.
- `services/api`: HTTPS API, SQLite persistence, workers, Agent Host bridge, Aether projection, and tests.
- `packages/cognition`: pure prompt/state transitions without database, timer, or network ownership.
- `packages/learning`: pure parts of Syzygy ADR-0008 stage 1 (switches, prediction ledger and calibration, reward and settlement rules, the value table and its versions, the value facts, the mood exit and its safeguards), without database, timer, network or environment; `exports: ./src/index.ts`. A new workspace package needs its `node_modules` link on the VM (a one-time `pnpm install --offline`).
- `packages/mind`: pure parts of Syzygy ADR-0008 stage 3 (switches, the `mind.event.v1` envelope, salience, the proactive gate, MinHash, evidence decay, identity tiers, temporal rules, compaction, the trust fold), without database, timer, network or environment; `exports: ./src/index.ts`; like `packages/learning` it needs its `node_modules` link on the VM.
- `services/api/src/mind/<part>/` and `services/api/src/infra/<layer>/`: the target layout of Syzygy ADR-0008 §6. New code of a part goes there from the start (`mind/learning`, `mind/inhibition`, `infra/models/jev`, `infra/sqlite`); existing flat files move only through the scripted layout track. Each folder is mapped to its core area in `autonomyCore.ts`.
- `deploy/azure`: production service templates.
- `deploy/macos`: Mac Agent Host launch configuration.
- `docs/architecture.md`: concise current architecture.
- `docs/DESIGN.md`: current design authority, evidence map, and screen-contract entrypoint.
- `docs/BRANCHING.md`: branch/worktree lifecycle and release-branch safety.
- `docs/self-improvement-runbook.md`: definition of done and recovery steps.

## Data ownership

- Azure SQLite is the durable source of truth for conversations, knowledge, memories, background activity, and agent tasks.
- The iOS conversation cache is an offline/display cache and must reconcile with the server without erasing newer local entries.
- Database changes must be additive and restart-safe. Back up the production database before a migration and verify row counts or health afterward.
- Secrets belong in ignored `.env` files, the Azure environment file, launchd environment, or iOS Keychain. Never copy secret values into source, logs, tests, prompts, or documentation.
- Cross-app payloads use versioned contracts and deterministic fixtures. If a change requires Aether, Voyager, or shared Syzygy ownership, do not silently edit a sibling repository: preserve compatibility, identify the owning contract, and return the decision to the coordinating task.

## Working method

1. Inspect `git status` before editing. The worktree may contain valuable unfinished user changes; preserve unrelated work.
2. Resolve the requested surface to its owning layer, then read the relevant current source and tests.
3. Implement a coherent vertical slice. Do not report a partial UI or a compile-only result as complete.
4. Run `scripts/verify.sh fast` during work, the narrowest useful target next, then `scripts/verify.sh all` for cross-layer work.
5. Open only `Shannon.xcworkspace` in Xcode. Never open the iOS and Mac `.xcodeproj` files in separate Xcode windows; both consume the same local `ShannonAppCore` package.
6. If the user requests Azure or physical-device delivery, follow the runbook and complete that delivery. Do not uninstall the app as part of an update.
7. If an external prerequisite is unavailable, leave the durable task state accurate and name the exact remaining step.
